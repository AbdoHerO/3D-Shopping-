# Reference photos of the real shop (not committed)

The 3D boutique was rebuilt from six photographs of a real perfumery supplied by the owner
(façade, interior, left, back and right walls, and a 3D top-down concept), plus the visual-planning
PDF. They show another shop's sign and phone number, and this repository is public, so the photos
are kept out of git (`.gitignore`). Put copies in this folder to work from them locally.

What was taken from them (all as estimates, in `SHOP` at the top of `src/lib/layout.ts`):

- black shopfront with a raised lit sign, soffit downlights, a window display on each side of the door;
- grey and white checkered floor, white 60 cm ceiling grid with round downlights;
- tall white shelving on the left wall, the back wall over a light-oak base cabinet, and the right wall;
- a glass-topped counter on an oak base along the right wall, till at its back end;
- a freestanding stepped nail-polish stand near the entrance; no central island.
